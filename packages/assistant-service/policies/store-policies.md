# mercato Store Policies

> Sample content for this learning project (feature 011, D-016). These are not the terms of a real store.
> The shopping assistant answers policy questions from this file only. Edit it and restart the assistant service
> to change the answers.

## Returns

- You can return an item within 30 days of delivery.
- The item must be unused and in its original packaging.

## Shipping

- Shipping is free on orders over $50. Orders of $50 or less pay a flat $5 shipping fee.
- Delivery takes 3–5 business days.
- We ship to domestic addresses only. We do not ship internationally.

## Warranty

- Every product comes with a 1-year warranty.

## Payment

- We accept card payments only. We don't accept any other payment method.
- Your card is charged when your order is confirmed, not when you place it.

## Cancellation and refunds

- You can cancel an order until it ships. Once an order has shipped, it can no longer be cancelled.
- Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5–7 business days. For a return, the 5–7 days
  start when we receive the returned item.
